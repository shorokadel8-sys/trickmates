How to Play

  1. Open the Toolbox T and pick a prank.
  2. Walk to the target object (follow the yellow arrow) and hold E to prepare it.
  3. Don't get caught! If your partner is close while you prepare, you lose points.
  4. The prank fires when your partner uses the object, walks by, or when you press Activate (remote pranks).
  5. Do your chores (hold E) for points — but watch out for traps.
  6. Suspicious? Hold F to search an object and defuse a hidden prank.
  7. Q rings a fake doorbell in another room to lure your partner.
